Intended use

MukhdaX is a provenance instrument: it examines an image, traces matching material on the public web, and anchors a fingerprint of that evidence on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. It is built for provenance research and evidence review.

What MukhdaX is not

  • It does not verify or establish any person's identity.
  • Provenance evidence is not proof of authenticity.
  • A recorded fingerprint proves that evidence existed in the stated form at the stated time; it says nothing about whether the depicted content is true.

Acceptable use

  • Only submit material you have the right to have processed.
  • Do not submit illegal content.
  • Do not abuse the service: automated bulk submission, attempts to exhaust the paid-search budget, or interference with the service may be rate-limited or refused.

Limitations

  • Discovery depends on an external reverse-image-search provider and the public pages it can see. There is no guarantee of search completeness: absence of matches is not proof of absence.
  • Metadata is whatever the discovered pages publicly expose and may be wrong, stale, or manipulated.
  • On-chain recording writes to a public test network and stores a digest only; a submitted transaction is not the same as a verified read-back, and the interface reports the two separately.
  • The service may be unavailable, slow, or capacity-limited. Paid and external dependencies can fail independently of MukhdaX itself.
